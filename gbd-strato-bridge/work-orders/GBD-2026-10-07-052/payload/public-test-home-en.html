Notice: Games Behind Doors is currently under construction and in a public testing phase. The website can already be visited, but individual functions are still being tested. When this notice no longer appears on a future visit, regular registration has been released. Thank you for your understanding.

Player accounts, email confirmation and password reset are being tested first. PayPal LIVE and real-money payments remain completely disabled.
