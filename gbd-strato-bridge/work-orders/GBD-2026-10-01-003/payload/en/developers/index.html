Submit your Android game.

Games Behind Doors is built for independent Android developers. Game submission stays free. Every submission goes through a manual review before publication.

Sign in / Create account View review process
REQUIREMENTS

What you need

A game file as APK, ZIP, HTML or HTM, a wide cover image, a square app logo, a description, minimum Android version and optionally up to six screenshots. The uploaded source file remains private. ZIP, HTML and HTM source packages are never delivered to players as developer archives. After review, the package processor creates a controlled, validated installation artifact; only that artifact may later be provided for installation. If no valid installation artifact can be produced, the game is not published. If your game processes personal data, you also need a reachable privacy policy. You must own or control the necessary rights to the game and media.

COST

Submission stays free

There is no fee for uploading or reviewing a game. Free games without purchases carry no sales commission. For paid games, in-app purchases, subscriptions or passes, the concrete PayPal and commission terms are shown after the corresponding pricing model is selected in the submission form. PayPal is the only supported payment provider for these transactions, and the seller account must be fully connected before publication.

PROCESS

From game file to publication

1Account & details
Confirm your email and enter the game details.
2Upload files
APK, ZIP, HTML or HTM plus cover image, app logo and optional screenshots are initially stored privately.
3Manual review
We review the submission under the published review process.
4Approval
Only approved games appear in the public catalog. Paid games and games with platform-processed in-app purchases, subscriptions or passes also require an active PayPal seller connection.
5Updates
New game versions can be submitted for review again.
SELLER & PAYPAL

Who receives the money?

For a paid game, the developer remains the seller of that game. Before publication, the developer connects a PayPal seller account to Games Behind Doors. The payment is assigned to the seller while Games Behind Doors receives the agreed 10% platform commission.

IN-APP PURCHASES

The same rule applies to platform purchases

In-app purchases, subscriptions and passes processed through Games Behind Doors are also subject to the 10% platform commission, separate PayPal fees and the requirement for an active PayPal seller connection. A monetized offer is not published without these requirements.

What games fit?

Android indie games across genres such as action, adventure, arcade, horror, racing, simulation, strategy and puzzle. Stolen content, malware and unlawful functionality are not accepted.
