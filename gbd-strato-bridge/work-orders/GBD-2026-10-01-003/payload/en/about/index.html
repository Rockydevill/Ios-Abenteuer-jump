Why Games Behind Doors?

Operator: RockydevgamesAndroid indie games

A platform for independent Android games

Games Behind Doors is being built to make smaller Android games and their developers easier to discover. Instead of publishing every upload automatically, the platform uses a manual approval process and clear game pages.

Who operates the platform?

Games Behind Doors is operated by Rockydevgames. Legal operator and contact information is available in the imprint.

What does “manually reviewed” mean?

A submission is checked against a documented review process before publication. The review cannot guarantee that a game is entirely bug-free or free of every unknown risk.

See the full review process.

How are games selected?

A game must follow the platform rules, have the necessary rights and pass the technical and content review. Publication is not guaranteed.

Contact

Questions about the platform, submissions or published games can be sent to rockydevgames@gmail.com.
