Manual and technical review can reduce identifiable risks but does not guarantee that a game is error-free, secure or compatible with every device.

3. Installation and use of games

The respective developer remains responsible for the content, operation and required rights of a game. Developer source packages such as ZIP, HTML or HTM files and private upload archives are not delivered to players. Players receive access only to the technically validated installation artifact assigned to an approved release. Users must review the information provided for each game before installation or use.

4. Paid content and payment processing

Live sales through Games Behind Doors are not currently enabled. Before any live-money activation, the seller/contracting role, invoicing and refund responsibility, VAT/OSS treatment and the planned PayPal marketplace flow must be separately reviewed. Games Behind Doors does not currently make a blanket statement that a particular party is always the seller or always solely responsible for tax.

Where a monetized offer is later enabled, trusted product data, the gross price and the currency are determined by the server. Games Behind Doors uses EUR as its marketplace currency. The client cannot set the trusted purchase price. A completed PayPal capture must match the expected gross amount and EUR currency before access is granted.

Before live sales are enabled, the checkout must clearly identify the offer, final price in EUR, relevant seller/provider information, whether the purchase is one-off or recurring, material functionality and compatibility information, cancellation terms where relevant, and the applicable withdrawal information. Until these reviews and feature gates are completed, real paid purchases cannot be completed through Games Behind Doors.

We aim to operate the service reliably but do not guarantee uninterrupted availability. Maintenance, security measures or technical faults may temporarily restrict the service.

6. EU service region

At the initial functional launch, public information pages may be readable worldwide. Accounts, submissions, downloads/installations and commercial functions are restricted to EU Member States. Users outside the EU remain read-only for the initial launch. Language and currency settings do not establish country eligibility.

7. Digital content and withdrawal information

Games Behind Doors does not use a blanket statement that digital content can never be withdrawn from. Where immediate supply of paid digital content is legally relevant, the checkout is designed to obtain a separate, non-preselected request for immediate supply and a separate acknowledgement of the legally relevant consequence before the order is created. The final consumer wording remains subject to review before live paid operation.

7a. Purchase confirmation

After a successfully confirmed purchase, Games Behind Doors may provide a server-generated purchase confirmation in the user account containing a receipt reference, the purchased content, seller information, the gross amount in EUR and the consents recorded for that purchase. Email delivery of this confirmation is planned only after the email service used for it has been technically verified and approved.

Duplicate provider events must not create duplicate grants, credits or charges. The detailed financial allocation between seller, payment provider and platform remains subject to the applicable payment and contractual rules.

Identity documents must not be requested as ordinary email attachments when a secure upload workflow is available. Reports of unlawful, dangerous or rights-infringing content can also be sent to rockydevgames@gmail.com.
