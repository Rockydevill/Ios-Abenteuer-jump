Developer Terms

Version 2026-10-01-v5Submission remains free of charge

1. Submission

Submitting a game to Games Behind Doors is permanently free of charge. There is no upload or review fee. Every submission is manually reviewed before any public publication. There is no entitlement to approval or publication.

2. Developer rights

For each submission, the developer confirms that they are entitled to provide the game file, texts, logos, cover images, screenshots and other content and that doing so does not infringe third-party rights.

The developer grants Games Behind Doors the non-exclusive rights required to review, store, technically provide and publicly present an approved game. All other rights to the game remain with the developer.

3. Prohibited content

Prohibited content includes malware, deliberately deceptive functionality, unlawful content, stolen content or content submitted without sufficient rights, and functionality designed to unlawfully spy on or harm users or their devices. Games Behind Doors may reject submissions or suspend or remove publications where security, legal or policy concerns exist.

4. Updates

New versions may be reviewed again. Approval of an earlier version does not mean later versions can be published without review.

5. Monetization, PayPal and platform commission

The 10% platform commission applies only to paid games and to in-app purchases, subscriptions and passes processed through Games Behind Doors. No sales commission applies to games that are entirely free and contain no purchase options. The 10% rule is shown and accepted in the submission form only when a paid game or an offer with in-app purchases, subscriptions or passes is selected.

Games Behind Doors currently plans to support PayPal as the payment provider for monetized offers. Google Play Billing, Klarna and other payment providers are not supported for sales processed through Games Behind Doors. Before publication, the developer must connect a PayPal seller account through the provided onboarding flow and be eligible to receive payments.

For recurring subscriptions, the 10% platform commission applies to each recurring payment processed through Games Behind Doors; for time-limited or one-time paid passes, it applies to each relevant purchase. Recurring payments will only be offered once PayPal has enabled the required platform capability for Games Behind Doors. PayPal fees, taxes, refunds and legally required deductions are handled separately from the 10% platform commission. Live payment processing will only be enabled after the required PayPal platform approval and successful technical testing.

6. Complete monetization disclosure

Each submission must fully and accurately disclose its pricing model, including real-money shops, in-app purchases, subscriptions, battle passes, season passes and other passes, premium currencies, coin packs, premium content, DLC-like content, external purchase pages or payment links, donations, tips and other real-money features.

If “other real-money feature” is selected, the feature must also be described. If a game is submitted as free but an undisclosed purchase or payment feature is found during review, the game will initially not be published. The developer will generally receive a “correction required” status and may correct and resubmit it. Repeated false disclosures may lead to rejection or removal.

7. Developer and seller data

For paid offers, in-app purchases, subscriptions and passes, additional identity, business-status and legally required seller information may be requested before publication. PayPal integration also requires technical seller data such as merchant identification, onboarding status, permission status and whether payments can be received. A monetized offer cannot be enabled without the required information and an active seller connection.

8. Game privacy

The developer must state whether the game processes personal data, for example through user accounts, analytics, advertising, cloud functionality or payments. If personal data is processed, a reachable privacy policy for the game must be supplied before publication and must reflect the actual functionality and services used.

Missing or clearly inaccurate privacy information may lead to a correction request, rejection or temporary removal of a published game.

9. Supported game files and technical processing

APK, ZIP, HTML and HTM files are currently accepted for submission up to the configured maximum size. The developer's original upload is stored privately and is used only for review and technical processing.

ZIP, HTML and HTM source packages and other developer archives are not provided as player downloads. After successful review, Games Behind Doors must create a separate, controlled and validated installation artifact or validate an already suitable Android artifact. Only that approved release artifact may later be delivered to players. If no valid installation artifact can be created or validated, the game is not published. Submitted HTML or HTM source code is not exposed as public developer source under the gamesbehinddoors.de origin.

10. Advertising

Developers must disclose whether their game contains advertising. Website advertising by Games Behind Doors is separate from advertising inside a submitted game. No participation in advertising revenue from third-party games exists unless a separate GBD advertising model is explicitly agreed in the future.

11. Review and removal

Games Behind Doors may analyse technical information from submitted game files for security and quality review. A review does not guarantee that a game is completely error-free or free from previously unknown risks. If issues later become known, a game may be temporarily blocked or removed.

12. Account and deletion

Developers may request deletion of their account. Account deletion does not automatically result in immediate deletion of all data where information must still be retained for published games, open matters, security evidence or legal retention duties.

13. EU service region and account eligibility

At the initial functional launch, developer accounts, submissions, downloads/installations and commercial platform functions are restricted to EU Member States. Public information pages may remain readable outside the EU. Language, currency and browser settings are not used as proof of country eligibility; server-side country signals and the developer's declared residence or business country are checked separately.

A country mismatch is not automatically treated as fraud. Travel, VPNs, mobile routing or geolocation errors may require additional verification. Until resolved, the affected functional or commercial action remains blocked.

14. Free versus monetized developers

A genuinely free game with no purchase, subscription, paid DLC, premium currency, external purchase button, developer tip/donation function or developer advertising revenue may be submitted by an eligible EU developer without an identity-document copy. Manual game, rights, security and quality review still applies.

Paid games, in-app purchases, subscriptions, passes, real-money currencies, developer tips/donations, external real-money purchase flows and developer advertising revenue require an additional commercial identity and seller verification before the monetized function may be approved.

15. Commercial verification and official identity evidence

Commercial verification is separate from platform administration and never grants administrator rights. The developer must provide the required legal identity/contact information, confirm age 18+, identify the seller role and, where required, provide an official identity document or suitable electronic identification. A private individual is not automatically treated as a trader merely because an identity document is verified.

Identity documents are kept in private storage, are not made publicly accessible and are reviewed only by authorised internal administrators. Raw documents are generally deleted after the verification purpose has been completed unless a specific retention reason has been documented. Games Behind Doors does not require an identity-document copy solely for a completely free, non-monetized hobby game.

16. Ranking, restrictions and platform treatment

The current default catalogue ranking is based primarily on publication recency. Paid ranking advantages are disabled. If ranking parameters materially change, the principal parameters will be described transparently without disclosing security-sensitive details that would facilitate manipulation.

Submissions or accounts may be restricted, suspended or rejected for documented security, rights, policy, verification or contractual reasons. Games owned by the platform operator are not given a hidden paid or ownership-based ranking advantage.

17. Commercial legal and tax review before live money

Before live paid operation, the seller/contracting role, invoice and refund responsibilities, tax/VAT/OSS treatment, withdrawal wording, PayPal split flow and any recurring-payment rules must be separately reviewed and approved. These matters are not inferred merely from the technical marketplace design. Until the relevant approvals are completed, the affected real-money functions remain disabled.

18. Changes

A new version number is used for material changes. New submissions may require acceptance of the current version. Where applicable, material changes affecting commercial developers will be communicated in a way consistent with the rules that apply to the relationship.
