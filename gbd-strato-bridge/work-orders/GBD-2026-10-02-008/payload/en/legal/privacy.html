Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-01-v4Last updated 1 October 2026

1. Controller

Rockydevgames, owner Florian Odebrecht, Klein Nieköhr 3, 17179 Behren-Lübchin, Germany.
Contact: rockydevgames@gmail.com

2. Website hosting

The website infrastructure is provided through STRATO. When the service is accessed, technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time, requested resource, browser information and technical logs may be processed for secure and reliable operation. Backend, authentication and database functions are additionally provided through Supabase.

Where applicable, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is secure operation and error analysis.

3. Developer accounts and authentication

Developer accounts use Supabase Auth. Data processed includes in particular email address, authentication information, account ID, confirmation status and technically necessary session information. Games Behind Doors does not store passwords in plain text.

Google OAuth may additionally be offered. When used, the account and identification data required for sign-in are processed between Google, Supabase and Games Behind Doors. Google's own processing is also governed by Google's privacy terms.

The processing serves authentication, account security and access to the developer area and is based in particular on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

4. Game submissions

For a game submission, we process the contact information and game details supplied by the developer as well as APK, ZIP, HTML or HTM files, cover images, app logos and optional screenshots. For abuse prevention and rate limiting, a pseudonymous hash derived from the source IP may also be stored.

During manual review, technical information about the submitted file may be evaluated, including file format, package and version information, signature data, Android permissions, integrated libraries, domains, network destinations and indicators of advertising, shops or payment functionality. Review status and correction notes may also be stored.

Developers must state whether their game processes personal data. Where it does, the privacy-policy URL provided for the game is stored and reviewed. Data is processed for review, communication with the developer and, where applicable, publication. Files that are not approved remain in the private review area.

4a. Country availability and abuse prevention

For game submissions, PayPal seller onboarding and other commercial actions, the server may determine the country associated with the current IP connection. Games Behind Doors uses the country code to check regional availability and to prevent misuse. The decision is performed server-side and is not based on browser language, currency or time zone.

The system normally stores only the declared country code, the detected IP country code, the check time and the result. A pseudonymous security hash may be stored where necessary for abuse prevention. Full IP addresses are not stored solely for the country check. Country-check records are currently configured for a maximum operational retention period of 180 days unless a longer period is required for a specific security incident or legal obligation.

No GPS location and no smartphone location permission are required for this check. A country mismatch is not treated automatically as fraud because VPNs, travel, mobile routing or geolocation errors can produce legitimate differences. Commercial functions are blocked or sent for additional verification when the country cannot be confirmed.

5. Email delivery

Resend may be used as a technical email provider for certain system and review messages. Data required for delivery is processed for that purpose.

6. Language and local settings

The selected site language and certain necessary settings may be stored locally in the browser to provide the function requested by the user.

7. Google AdSense

Google AdSense is currently not active on Games Behind Doors. No Google advertising is loaded through the prepared AdSense integration and no AdSense publisher ID is currently transmitted to Google through it.

Before any future activation, a consent-management solution suitable for Google will be implemented. Advertising will only be enabled after the required configuration, site approval and consent management are complete. This Privacy Policy will then be updated accordingly.

8. PayPal, seller onboarding and payments

For paid games and for in-app purchases, subscriptions and passes processed through Games Behind Doors, the developer must connect an eligible PayPal seller account before publication. The connection uses PayPal's seller onboarding. A monetized offer is not published without an active PayPal connection. PayPal is currently the only payment provider planned for these platform payments; Games Behind Doors does not use Google Play Billing, Klarna or other providers for them.

PayPal onboarding and later payment processing may involve data such as PayPal merchant identification, onboarding and permission status, primary-email confirmation, payment-receiving eligibility and technical risk or consent status. Games Behind Doors does not receive the developer's PayPal password or bank-account login details.

Purchases, in-app purchases, subscriptions and passes may also involve transaction information such as order and payment identifiers, gross amount, currency, Games Behind Doors platform commission, PayPal fees, developer net allocation and payment, refund or cancellation status. This supports payment processing, allocation of the agreed 10% platform commission, accounting, refunds and transaction traceability.

PayPal additionally processes data under its own responsibility and privacy terms. Where processing is necessary for the seller and platform relationship, it is carried out in particular for contractual performance. Statutory retention and evidence obligations remain unaffected.

Live payments are not currently enabled. Payment functionality will only be enabled after PayPal platform approval and successful technical testing of developer share, 10% platform commission and payment-provider fees.

9. Commercial identity and seller verification

Where a developer requests monetization, additional commercial-verification data may be processed, including legal name, address, contact information, age-18 confirmation, seller type, business information, verification status and, where required, an official identity document or suitable electronic identification. Commercial verification is separate from administrator access and never grants platform-administrator rights.

Identity files are stored only in a private storage area and are not exposed through permanent public URLs. Access by an authorised administrator is logged and, where a temporary viewing link is required, it is short-lived. Games Behind Doors does not perform blanket OCR collection of the entire document. After successful verification, raw identity files are generally deleted once they are no longer required for the verification purpose unless a specific retention reason has been documented.

10. Security, rate limits and logs

For authentication, uploads, downloads, support, payment-related actions and abuse prevention, technical security information may be processed. Depending on the function this may include timestamps, request counters, pseudonymous security hashes, country codes, review/audit events and technical error information. Full IP addresses are not retained merely for country checking. Access to identity documents and sensitive administrative decisions is separately audited.

11. Retention and deletion

Different data categories have separate retention rules; Games Behind Doors does not apply one blanket period to all data. Country-check records are currently configured for an operational period of up to 180 days. Raw identity documents are designed to be deleted after completed verification unless a documented reason requires temporary retention. Concrete retention periods for payment/accounting records, consent/contract evidence, rejected submissions, support cases and security logs remain subject to the applicable legal and operational review before the corresponding live functions are enabled.

An account-deletion request may result in deletion or anonymisation of profile data, revocation of sessions and entitlements, and deletion of private identity documents. Records that must be retained for unresolved publications, payment evidence, security incidents or statutory duties are kept separately and are not promised to be erased immediately.

12. Recipients and service providers

Depending on the function actually used, data may be processed by STRATO for hosting, Supabase for backend/authentication/database/storage functions, Resend for email delivery and PayPal for seller onboarding and payments. Advertising services are not listed as active recipients while advertising remains disabled. Each provider processes data within the role and purpose applicable to the concrete service.

13. Data subject rights

Subject to the applicable legal requirements, data subjects may have rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Consent may be withdrawn for the future. Data subjects also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection supervisory authority.

14. Privacy settings

Optional advertising technologies are currently disabled. The technical preparation is designed to prevent AdSense from being started accidentally before the required configuration is complete.

Ongoing development

This statement reflects the current technical state. It will be updated before additional payment, advertising or tracking services are enabled.
